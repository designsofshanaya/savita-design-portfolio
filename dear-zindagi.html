A student-shot introduction inspired by a favourite film—chosen because its story felt personally relatable.

Filming the introductory sequence

Playback and audio start only when you use the video controls.

01 / VISUAL DESCRIPTION

A quiet moment.
A change of scene.

The opening follows a woman resting in bed, with a soft toy nearby and light coming through a window. A closer shot shows her waking and moving the blanket.

A later shot moves to a sofa by a window, where she sits holding a cup. The scenes use domestic spaces, soft light, and changes in camera distance to establish an everyday mood.

The supplied video contains visible credits and a Red Chillies Entertainment source mark.

02 / THE BRIEF & MY CONTRIBUTION

A favourite film.
A personal connection.

Our teacher asked us to shoot an introduction for our favourite movie. I chose Dear Zindagi because the film felt relatable to me.

I filmed this introduction while learning photography and videography. The assignment gave me a way to explore how framing, light, and movement turn an everyday moment into a visual story.

The film is the inspiration

This is a student recreation inspired by Dear Zindagi, rather than an official introduction or commissioned film work. The reference-film source mark visible in the export is retained.

What I would explore next

Compare a close framing with a wider scene: how does the camera distance change the feeling of the moment? Then experiment with the order of shots to see how the same material can tell a different story.
