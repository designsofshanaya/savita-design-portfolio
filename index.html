BEHIND KEYMITRA / DRM GROUP PROJECTAround thirty conversations.
One new possibility.Open the research story ↗

Our DRM (Design Research Methodologies) group project took us to around 30 keymakers across Mumbai. Primary and secondary research led to a How Might We question, more exploration, and an idea for keeping customer records.

My part
I took part in the interviews and research, and brought the customer-record concept to life as KeyMitra.

What I loved? Seeing real conversations turn into something we could make.

Hi, I’m Savita Wahade! I’m a B.Des student at Somaiya School of Design in Mumbai, exploring Product Design, UI/UX, and Graphic Design. What excites me most? Finding an idea in something ordinary and seeing where it takes me.

I love the moment when a project starts to come alive—when the colours click, a layout feels right, or an interaction becomes easier to use. I enjoy exploring different ways to get there, trying new tools, and discovering something I hadn’t expected.

Each project here holds a little of that excitement: an app that grew out of design research, a film made while learning photography and videography, and a chance to explore what AI tools can create. I’d love to show you the work and tell you what I discovered along the way.
