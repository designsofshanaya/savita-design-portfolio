BEHIND KEYMITRA / DRM GROUP PROJECTAround thirty conversations.
One new possibility.Open the research story ↗

KeyMitra began as a DRM team project with field research among roadside keymakers in Mumbai. We studied how their workspace, tools, materials, and manual records shaped their daily work.

My role — UI/UX and app design
I translated the team’s research insights into user flows, wireframes, and interface designs tailored to keymakers’ needs.

What we found

  • Compact workspaces made organisation important.
  • Tools and key blanks were not always easy to access; repeated searching interrupted work.
  • Manual customer records took time to store and retrieve.

How might we?

How might we help keymakers organise their limited workspace so they can easily access key blanks, tools, and materials while working?

How might we help keymakers digitally record and retrieve essential customer and key details efficiently while minimising physical storage?

Solution and testing

The app concept brings customer and key information into one place and supports key-blank inventory. We tested the prototype with keymakers, using feedback to refine its usability and fit with their daily workflow.

Quicker retrieval, less paper, and easier record management are intended benefits; no numerical outcomes are claimed.
